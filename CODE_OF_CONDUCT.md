# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Domain Footprint Atlas welcoming, respectful, and professional for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted attention.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging unsafe, illegal, or non-passive use of the project.
- Any conduct that maintainers reasonably consider inappropriate in a professional open source community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior that is harmful, inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or unsafe.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through GitHub issues when the report is non-sensitive. For sensitive reports, use private repository contact methods when available.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated with respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter and affected community members.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other public communication channels connected to Domain Footprint Atlas.
